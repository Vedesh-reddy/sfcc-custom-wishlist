for Salesforce Storefront Reference Architecture (SFRA) storefronts. It is an SFRA
extension, not an official Salesforce product.

`server` module, middleware, `emailHelpers`, page layout, the
`product/components/addToCartButtonExtension.isml` extension point, and the
